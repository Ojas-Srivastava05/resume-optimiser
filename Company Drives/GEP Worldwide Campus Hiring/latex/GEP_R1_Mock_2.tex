\section{Script}

\subsection*{0:00 --- Intro}
\IV{Hi Ojas. Quick intro --- 60 seconds.}
\You{Locked intro, same content, slightly tighter pace. Still hit LogiFlow, Community Hero, Nexus, GEP bridge. Stop.}

\subsection*{2:00 --- OOP keywords}
\IV{Access specifiers --- public, private, protected. Where do you use private?}
\You{Fields private; behaviour through methods. LogiFlow: cache keys and DB handles not exposed --- callers use \texttt{getRoutes()} only.}

\IV{What does static mean? Same as constant?}
\You{Belongs to class, not instance. Static method cannot touch instance fields. \textbf{Not} constant.}

\IV{Abstraction vs encapsulation --- people confuse these.}
\You{Abstraction hides complexity (what vs how). Encapsulation bundles data and restricts access. Different ideas.}

\IV{Design a bank account in OOP --- 90 seconds, classes only.}
\You{Abstract \texttt{Account}: balance, \texttt{deposit()}, \texttt{withdraw()}. \texttt{Savings}/\texttt{Current} override withdraw rules. \texttt{Transaction} for audit. Balance private.}

\subsection*{8:00 --- LogiFlow (resume-driven)}
\IV{Redis on your resume. Explain exactly how you used it.}
\You{Cache-aside on hot corridor reads. Key from query params. TTL. Invalidate on write. SQL source of truth.}

\IV{Two users, same corridor, both cache miss --- problem?}
\You{Both recompute --- wasteful but OK for reads. Thundering herd possible; could add lock or single-flight. Not inventory-critical like cart stock.}

\IV{Which algorithms? Do not say ML unless you can defend it.}
\You{Graph over corridor network. BFS when hops uniform; weighted shortest path when not. Scoring on time, cost, delay.}

\IV{Favourite data structure?}
\You{Graph --- stations nodes, corridors edges. Adjacency list (sparse). BFS for unweighted / level-order. Stay on graph if they grill internals.}

\subsection*{14:00 --- DSA}
\IV{Array and target --- return indices of two numbers that sum to target. One pass.}
\You{For each \texttt{x}, check \texttt{target - x} in map \textbf{before} inserting \texttt{x}. Avoids using same element twice. O(n) time, O(n) space.}

\noindent{\small\textbf{Why check before insert?} For \texttt{[3,3]}, target 6 --- cannot use index 0 twice.}

\subsection*{18:00 --- Close}
\IV{Why GEP?}
\You{Procurement + supply chain --- operational data to a decision. Same shape as LogiFlow. SMART, NEXXE, Quantum Intelligence --- product plus consulting judgement.}
