\subsection*{10:00 --- OOP (compressed)}

\You{Composition --- Ranker \emph{has} pipelines, does not inherit. Easier to swap railway for hybrid at runtime.}

\IV{Overloading vs overriding?}
\You{Overloading = compile-time, different signatures. Overriding = runtime, subclass replaces parent.}
