The four modules

#ModuleNameTimeWhat you do
APersonalityADEPT-1525–30 min Pick which of two statements is more like you. Adaptive. No right/wrong.
BInductivescales ix5 min 20 tasks. Nine objects — find the odd one out.
CDeductivegapChallenge6 min Unlimited grids. Fill the ? cell. Latin-square rule.
DPlanningmotionChallenge6 min Unlimited puzzles. Move deflectors so the ball reaches the exit.
Personality usually comes first. Cognitive modules are short and timed. You may pause between modules, never mid-module.

What each module feels like

A. ADEPT-15

  • Forced-choice pairs about workplace behaviour
  • Measures 15 traits across 6 styles
  • No correct answer — consistency matters
  • Answer from one stable work self, not an idealised self

B. Inductive (scales ix)

  • Nine objects labelled A–I
  • Eight follow one rule; one does not
  • Target pace: ~15 seconds per task

C. gapChallenge

  • 4×4 or 5×5 grid of symbols; one cell is ?
  • Rule: each symbol appears once per row and once per column
  • Score = how many correct in 6 minutes

Legend used in this guide: O = circle · # = square · ^ = triangle · * = star · + = cross · . = empty · ? = solve

D. motionChallenge

  • 6×6 grid · columns A–F · rows 1–6
  • \ turns: East→South, South→East, West→North, North→West
  • / turns: East→North, North→East, West→South, South→West
  • # blocker stops the ball · walls stop the ball
  • One move = relocate one deflector · fewer moves is better

Critical rules

RuleWhat it means
No mid-task exitClosing mid-module usually forfeits it. No reattempt.
20-min idle logoutKeep breaks between modules under ~10 minutes.

2. Sample Questions

2.1 ADEPT-15 — 8 sample pairs

Choose A or B — whichever is more like you at work.

#Statement AStatement B
1I keep a detailed written plan for every project.I adjust my approach as soon as new information arrives.
2I speak up early when I disagree with a decision.I make sure everyone has had their say before I add mine.
3I stay calm when a deadline slips.I push hard to recover the lost time.
4I enjoy debating abstract ideas and models.I prefer to test an idea in practice straight away.
5I notice quickly when a colleague is frustrated.I stay focused on the task until it is complete.
6I credit the team before I mention my own part.I make sure my contribution is visible to decision-makers.
7I bring energy and talk freely in meetings.I read my own reactions accurately under pressure.
8I keep a positive outlook when a plan fails.I re-forecast realistically as soon as a plan fails.
No answer key. Both options sound good on purpose. Stay consistent across similar themes.

2.4 motionChallenge — 3 samples

M-S1

Ball at A1 heading East → Exit F6. Fixed \ at C1. Movable \ at E4. Blockers at D2, B5.

Answer: 1 move — place \ at C6. Path: A1 → C1 ↓ C6 → F6 EXIT.

M-S2

Ball A1 East → Exit A6. Movable \ at C2 and / at B5. Blockers B4, C4, D5, F3.

Answer: 2 moves — \ → E1, / → E6. Path: A1 → E1 ↓ E6 ← A6 EXIT.

M-S3

Answer: 2 moves — \ → C5, / → F5. Path: C1 ↓ C5 → F5 ↑ F1 EXIT.

6. Day-of Checklist & Strategy

24 hours before

  • □ Re-do official demos (~15 min)
  • □ Memorise \ and / turn rules
  • □ Sleep > cramming
  • □ Confirm HomeHub login (don’t start)
  • □ Block 60 minutes in calendar

30 minutes before

  • □ Laptop plugged in
  • □ Strong Wi-Fi / ethernet
  • □ Pop-ups allowed for aon-assessment.com
  • □ VPN / Slack / Teams closed
  • □ Phone in another room
  • □ Paper + pen ready

Strategy by module

ADEPT-15: Steady pace. One work context. Pick what’s more true, not more impressive.

Inductive: Scan count → sides → fill → orientation. Cap ~20s; guess and move on.

gapChallenge: Column → row → intersect missing sets. Don’t solve the whole grid. Aim ~20s/item.

motionChallenge: Plan backwards from the exit. Blockers reveal the corridor. Cap ~60s then take next.

If something breaks

  1. Don’t close unless clearly crashed
  2. Screenshot (include URL + clock)
  3. Check pop-up blocker first
  4. Email indiasupport@aon.com same day + notify recruiter
  5. Don’t restart the whole battery blindly
Final pre-flight: pop-ups on · VPN off · phone away · charger in · paper ready · deflection rules fresh · gapChallenge scan order fresh. Good luck.
